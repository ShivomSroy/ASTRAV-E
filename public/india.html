India first

Radar sees through monsoon clouds. This page shows how NISAR and other NASA data can help us understand floods, sinking ground and glacial lake risk in India.

Why radar matters

During the monsoon, clouds block normal satellite photos exactly when floods happen. Radar can see through them, but radar maps are hard to read. This page explains them in plain language.

It is for people such as district officials, teachers, local reporters and students in India. This is a prototype for explanation, not a flood warning system.
